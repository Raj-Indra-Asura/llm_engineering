# Grid Carbon Intensity and Emissions Accounting

Grid carbon intensity can be measured as average intensity or marginal intensity. Average intensity describes the mix of generation on the system, while marginal intensity estimates the emissions impact of the next unit of demand or export.

For the UK, a representative average grid intensity in 2024 is about 0.23 kgCO2/kWh, but the hourly value can range from roughly 0.05 kgCO2/kWh in very renewable periods to about 0.40 kgCO2/kWh in gas-heavy periods. These values are relevant for Scope 2 electricity accounting.

Battery storage can shift charging into lower-carbon hours and reduce imports during higher-carbon hours. Exported electricity can be credited at the export carbon intensity because it displaces other generation on the system.

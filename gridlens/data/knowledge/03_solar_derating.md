# Solar PV Performance and Derating Factors

Solar PV nameplate capacity is quoted under standard test conditions (STC): 1000 W/m² irradiance, 25°C cell temperature, and a standard spectrum. Real systems rarely operate exactly at STC.

In practice, the performance ratio (PR) of a PV system is commonly around 0.75 to 0.85 after accounting for temperature losses, inverter efficiency, wiring losses, mismatch, and soiling. A typical rule of thumb is that panel output drops by about 0.4% per °C above 25°C for many crystalline silicon modules.

Seasonal variation is large in the UK. January peak generation may be only about 1 to 2 kWh/kWp/day, while good summer days can reach about 5 to 6 kWh/kWp/day. Scenario models therefore need a derating factor instead of assuming nameplate output every hour.

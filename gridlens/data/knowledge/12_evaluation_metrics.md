# How to Evaluate Microgrid Performance

Solar fraction is the proportion of total demand served by solar energy, often expressed as solar kWh divided by total demand kWh. Self-sufficiency measures how much demand is met without net grid imports and can be written as 1 minus grid_import divided by demand.

Other common metrics include peak demand reduction, which shows how much the maximum site import or demand is lowered, and levelized cost of storage (LCOS), which spreads lifetime battery cost over delivered energy. Emissions intensity is total kgCO2 divided by total consumed kWh.

Analysts may also track carbon payback period, which estimates how long it takes an intervention to offset the emissions associated with manufacturing and deployment.

# Battery Sizing for Microgrids

Battery sizing is often described with an energy-to-power ratio. A 400 kWh battery with a 100 kW inverter provides four hours of storage at rated power. Campus and commercial microgrids commonly target about 2 to 4 hours of storage, depending on whether the goal is peak shaving, backup support, or solar self-consumption.

Peak shaving strategies prioritize reducing short high-demand periods, so they may value higher inverter power. Self-consumption strategies may prioritize energy capacity so that midday solar can be shifted into evening demand.

Economic payback depends on tariff spread, cycle frequency, battery efficiency, and asset lifetime. Solar-plus-storage projects also consider the solar capacity factor when estimating how often the battery can be charged from on-site generation.

# System Limitations and Known Constraints

1. Hourly resolution only - misses sub-hourly peaks and fast transients.
2. Single tariff zone - no locational pricing or nodal congestion modeling.
3. No network constraints - assumes unlimited grid connection.
4. Simplified battery model - no degradation, thermal effects, or C-rate limits.
5. Seasonal-naive forecast - poor accuracy during unusual weather or events.
6. No reactive power or power flow simulation.
7. Single asset optimization - no multi-battery or multi-inverter coordination.
8. No real-time control - this is a planning tool, not an operational EMS.
9. Not suitable for safety-critical grid operations.

# Grid Tariff Models for Microgrids

Flat rate tariffs charge the same import price in every hour of the day. For small and medium UK commercial sites, a representative flat retail benchmark is about £0.25/kWh, with a separate lower export payment for electricity sent back to the grid.

Time-of-use (TOU) tariffs vary by hour. A common simplified pattern is peak pricing around £0.40/kWh during evening or business peaks and off-peak pricing around £0.15/kWh overnight or midday when the system is less stressed. Feed-in tariffs or export rates reward grid exports, but they are usually lower than import prices.

Battery storage improves demand flexibility under TOU pricing by charging during low-price hours and discharging during peak-price hours. This can reduce total energy cost even if total electricity consumption is unchanged.

# Emissions Reduction Strategies for Campus Microgrids

The most direct way to reduce electricity emissions is to lower grid imports through solar self-consumption. On-site PV offsets purchased electricity and can cut both cost and Scope 2 emissions when it serves local demand.

Smart battery dispatch can further reduce emissions by charging during low-carbon hours and discharging during high-carbon hours. Demand response, flexible HVAC scheduling, and managed EV charging can reinforce this effect by moving consumption away from carbon-intensive periods.

Some campus systems also coordinate combined heat and power (CHP), thermal storage, and EV infrastructure. Those interactions can materially change the best emissions strategy even if the electrical model is simplified.

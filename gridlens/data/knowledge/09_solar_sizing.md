# Solar PV System Sizing

Solar PV sizing often starts with the relationship between installed kWp and annual energy yield in kWh. In the UK, annual yield depends on irradiance zone, orientation, shading, and system losses, so the same kWp can produce different yearly energy at different sites.

The oversizing ratio compares solar generation potential with site load. A higher solar-to-load ratio increases renewable contribution but may also increase exports or curtailment unless storage or flexible demand is available.

Optimal tilt and orientation are site-specific, though south-facing arrays with moderate tilt are common in the UK. Project developers may also compare on-site solar with off-site PPA options when physical roof or land area is constrained.
